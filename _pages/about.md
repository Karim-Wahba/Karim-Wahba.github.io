I'm a Machine Learning MSc student at the [University of Tübingen](https://uni-tuebingen.de/). My research interests lie at the intersection of visual representation learning, mechanistic interpretability, and causal AI. I am interested in how models represent the visual world, how these representations drive their predictions, and how temporal and interaction signals can reveal causal structure. My previous work has focused on temporal consistency and interpretability in vision-language models.
